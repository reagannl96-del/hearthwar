Hearthwar privacy policy

Hearthwar is a small browser game. This page explains what it keeps about you.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and a Google account ID. We use them only to recognise you in the game and show your ruler name. We never see your Google password.

The game stores your progress: your villages, troops, reports and the name you chose. Settings such as sound and the last map zoom stay in your own browser.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your information, show ads, or track you across other sites.

Where it lives

Sign-in and saved games are stored with Supabase. The website is hosted by Vercel and the game server by Render.

Deleting your data

Email the site owner at the address on the Google sign-in screen and your account and villages will be removed.
